# M-Pesa Float and Airtime Policy — Baraka General Store

**Last updated:** 2026-07-15
**Applies to:** all staff handling the M-Pesa till

## 1. Daily Float

1.1. The shop keeps an M-Pesa float of **KES 15,000** at the start of each trading day. The opening attendant confirms the balance against the till log before the first customer.

1.2. If the float falls below **KES 3,000** during a shift, notify the shop manager immediately so it can be topped up. Do not turn customers away without escalating first.

## 2. Commission

2.1. The shop earns commission on M-Pesa deposits and withdrawals according to the Safaricom agent tariff. Commission is reconciled weekly, every Monday, against the agent statement.

2.2. Commission earnings are **not** part of the daily sales total and must be recorded separately in the till log so they are not double counted in the turnover tax return.

## 3. Airtime

3.1. Airtime is sold at face value with no markup. The shop's margin comes from the Safaricom dealer discount, currently **4 percent**.

3.2. Airtime sales are recorded as ordinary sales, unlike M-Pesa commission.

## 4. Cash Limits

4.1. A single M-Pesa withdrawal above **KES 35,000** requires the shop manager's approval, in line with the authorization limits in the Employee Roles and Access Policy.

4.2. Never process a withdrawal for a customer who cannot produce the phone the transaction is registered to.
