# Pricing and Customer Discount Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-01-15
**Applies to:** all sales, wholesale and retail

## 1. Customer tiers

| Tier | Qualifying condition | Discount on total bill |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| Walk-in | No account, pays on the spot | 0% |
| Regular | Registered customer, 3+ purchases in the last 30 days | 3% |
| Wholesale | Buys 20kg or more of a single item in one visit | 7% |
| Partner | Written agreement with the shop (churches, schools, SACCOs) | 10% |

## 2. Stacking rules

2.1. Discounts do **not** stack. A customer qualifying for more than one tier receives only the highest single discount that applies.

2.2. Wholesale and Partner discounts apply to the qualifying item only, not the whole basket, unless the whole basket meets the same condition.

## 3. Rounding

3.1. Discounted totals are rounded down to the nearest shilling in the customer's favor.

## 4. Manager override

4.1. The shop manager may authorize an additional discount of up to 5% on top of the applicable tier discount for bulk clearance of near-expiry stock. This requires a manager's signature in the sales book and cannot be applied by staff without it.

## 5. Promotional pricing

5.1. Any promotional price posted in the shop overrides the tier discount for that specific item during the promotion period, and promotional prices do not stack with tier discounts.
