# Supplier Agreement: Unga Millers Ltd

**Parties:** Unga Millers Ltd ("Supplier") and Baraka General Store, Kawangware ("Retailer")
**Effective date:** 2026-03-01
**Products covered:** Unga wa ngano (wheat flour), unga wa mahindi (maize flour), sukari (sugar), mchele (rice)

## 1. Payment Terms

1.1. All deliveries are invoiced on a 14-day credit basis. Payment is due within **14 calendar days** of the delivery date shown on the invoice.

1.2. Late payment beyond 14 days attracts a penalty of 2% of the outstanding invoice value per week, capped at 10%.

1.3. Payment must be made via M-Pesa Paybill 400-200 or bank transfer to Unga Millers' account. Cash payments to delivery drivers are **not accepted** and will not be recognized as valid payment.

## 2. Returns and Damaged Stock

2.1. Damaged or torn packaging must be reported to the Supplier within **48 hours** of delivery, with photographic evidence sent via WhatsApp to the account manager.

2.2. Approved returns are credited against the next invoice, not refunded in cash.

2.3. Products past their printed expiry date at the time of delivery are replaced free of charge with no reporting deadline.

## 3. Minimum Order and Pricing

3.1. Minimum order per delivery is 10 bags (50kg each) per product line.

3.2. Prices are reviewed quarterly and communicated in writing at least 7 days before taking effect.

3.3. Orders above 50 bags in a single delivery qualify for a 5% volume discount, applied automatically on the invoice.

## 4. Delivery

4.1. Standard delivery window is 3 to 5 business days from order confirmation.

4.2. The Retailer must inspect and sign for the delivery at the point of handover. An unsigned delivery note voids any later damage claim.

## 5. Termination

5.1. Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.

5.2. Outstanding invoices remain payable under the terms above regardless of termination.

---
*Signed and effective as of the date above. Supersedes all prior verbal or written agreements between the parties regarding these product lines.*
