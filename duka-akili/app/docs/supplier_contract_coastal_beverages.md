# Supplier Agreement: Coastal Beverages Distributors

**Parties:** Coastal Beverages Distributors ("Supplier") and the Retailer
**Effective date:** 2026-05-20
**Products covered:** Soda (sodas and soft drinks), maji (bottled water), juice cartons

## 1. Payment Terms

1.1. All deliveries are **cash on delivery**. The delivery driver is authorized to collect cash or M-Pesa payment directly at the point of handover and will issue a signed receipt.

1.2. No credit terms are offered on any order under KES 20,000. Orders above this value may request 7-day credit subject to the Supplier's approval in writing.

## 2. Returns and Damaged Stock

2.1. Breakages or leaking bottles must be reported to the delivery driver immediately at the point of delivery. Claims made after the driver has left are not honored.

2.2. Approved breakage claims are replaced on the next delivery at no charge.

## 3. Minimum Order and Pricing

3.1. Minimum order is 5 crates per product line.

3.2. Empty crates and bottles must be returned on the next delivery. A deposit of KES 50 per crate is charged if empties are not returned within 30 days.

## 4. Delivery

4.1. Standard delivery window is 1 to 2 business days from order confirmation, reflecting the shorter shelf routes for beverages.

## 5. Termination

5.1. Either party may terminate this agreement with 14 days written notice.

---
*Signed and effective as of the date above.*
