# Staff Handbook — Baraka General Store

**Shop:** Baraka General Store, Kawangware, Nairobi
**Last updated:** 2025-08-10
**Applies to:** all staff

## 1. Welcome and Shop Hours

Baraka General Store is open Monday to Saturday, 6:30am to 9:00pm, and Sunday, 8:00am to 7:00pm. Staff on the opening shift must arrive 15 minutes before opening to set up the counter and check the previous night's till reconciliation.

## 2. Customer Service Standards

- Greet every customer within a few seconds of them reaching the counter.
- If you do not know the answer to a pricing or policy question, do not guess. Check with the shop manager or use Duka Akili to look it up before answering the customer.
- Regular and wholesale customers should be recognized and offered their correct discount tier without being asked to prove it every visit, once registered.

## 3. Cash Handling and Till Procedure

3.1. The till float at the start of each shift is KES 2,000. Count it with the outgoing staff member before they leave.

3.2. Any single cash sale above KES 5,000 should be confirmed against the receipt book before the customer leaves.

3.3. M-Pesa till number for the shop is separate from the owner's personal number. Never ask a customer to send payment to a personal number.

3.4. At the end of each shift, count the till, record the total in the till log, and leave a signed handover note for the next shift.

## 4. Handling Supplier Deliveries and Returns

### 4.1 Receiving a delivery

- Check the delivery note against what was actually ordered before signing anything.
- Count bags and inspect for torn or wet packaging while the driver is still present.

### 4.2 Damaged or torn stock

If any bags arrive damaged or torn, report it to the shop manager within **7 days** of delivery so it can be raised with the supplier for credit or replacement. Take a photo if possible, but a written note in the delivery log is acceptable if a phone is not available.

### 4.3 Paying delivery drivers

Some suppliers allow cash payment directly to the delivery driver against the invoice, especially for small or urgent top-up orders. Always ask for a signed receipt from the driver if paying cash on delivery. Check the specific supplier's contract on file first, since not every supplier accepts cash at the door.

### 4.4 Expired stock

Never accept stock that is already past its printed expiry date. Refuse the delivery at the door and note it in the delivery log.

## 5. Health and Safety

5.1. Keep the aisle in front of the cold storage unit clear at all times; it is also the fire exit route.

5.2. Report any spill immediately and mop it before continuing service, even if it means a short queue at the till.

5.3. The first aid kit is kept under the counter, next to the till log. Restock it from the owner if anything is used.

## 6. Who to Contact

For any delivery dispute, first contact the shop manager. If unresolved within 3 days, escalate to the owner directly. For questions about your own pay, shift schedule, or role, speak to the owner.
