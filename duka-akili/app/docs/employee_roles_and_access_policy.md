# Employee Roles and Access Policy — Baraka General Store

**Last updated:** 2026-07-01
**Applies to:** all staff and the Duka Akili assistant

## 1. Roles

Baraka General Store has three levels of staff:

- **Owner** — Faith Wanjiru. Full authority over the business: supplier agreements, margins, pricing changes, tax filings, hiring, and firing.
- **Shop Manager** — Peter Otieno. Runs day-to-day operations: authorizes manager override discounts, handles supplier delivery disputes escalated from staff, approves the staff shift schedule.
- **Shop Attendant** — Grace Achieng and Brian Mwangi. Serves customers at the counter: applies the standard discount tiers, receives deliveries, and escalates anything outside their authority to the shop manager.

## 2. Discount Authorization Limits

2.1. Any staff member may apply the standard tier discount from the Pricing and Discount Policy — walk-in, regular, wholesale, or partner — without approval, since these are fixed rates that do not require judgment.

2.2. Only the Shop Manager or the Owner may authorize the manager override of up to 5% described in the Pricing and Discount Policy, section 4. A shop attendant must never apply this override on their own signature, even for a customer they know well or a bag that is visibly close to expiry.

2.3. No one, including the Owner, may authorize a discount larger than the manager override cap without a written exception signed and dated, kept with the till log.

## 3. What Each Role May Ask Duka Akili

3.1. Shop attendants may ask about pricing tiers, return and refund windows, and general shop policy. They should not ask Duka Akili to disclose supplier payment terms, invoice values, or profit margins — those questions should go to the Shop Manager or Owner directly.

3.2. The Shop Manager may additionally ask about supplier credit terms, delivery dispute history, and discount override authorization.

3.3. Only the Owner should ask about tax filings, overall revenue, or the terms for renegotiating or terminating a supplier agreement.

## 4. Escalation

4.1. A shop attendant who is asked for a discount or exception beyond what section 2.1 permits must refer the customer to the Shop Manager, not decide it themselves and mention it later.

4.2. A delivery dispute that the Shop Manager cannot resolve within 3 days, per the Staff Handbook section 6, goes to the Owner.

## 5. Audit

5.1. Any discount override the Shop Manager authorizes should be recorded in the till log with the manager's initials, the customer, and the reason, so the Owner can review overrides during the monthly reconciliation.

---
*This policy states who is authorized to approve what. It does not, on its own, technically prevent a question from being answered by role — that access control is tracked as a roadmap item. Until then, staff are expected to follow the limits in this document themselves.*
