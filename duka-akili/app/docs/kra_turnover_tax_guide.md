# KRA Turnover Tax (TOT) Guide for Small Retailers

**Shop:** Baraka General Store, Kawangware, Nairobi
**Source basis:** Kenya Revenue Authority turnover tax guidance for small businesses.
**Applies to:** businesses with annual gross sales between KES 500,000 and KES 25,000,000, that are not VAT registered.

## 1. What Turnover Tax is

Turnover Tax (TOT) is charged on gross sales, not profit, at a rate of **3%** of monthly gross turnover. It replaces the need to calculate deductible expenses for tax purposes.

## 2. Filing deadline

2.1. TOT is filed and paid **monthly**, by the **20th day of the following month**. For example, July's turnover tax is due by 20 August.

2.2. Filing is done through the iTax portal. A KRA PIN is required to register.

## 3. Record keeping

3.1. Retailers must keep a daily record of gross sales, even informally in a ledger book, to support the monthly TOT return.

3.2. Records should be kept for a minimum of **5 years** in case of an audit.

## 4. Penalties

4.1. Late filing attracts a penalty of the higher of 5% of the tax due or KES 2,000.

4.2. Late payment attracts a further 1% interest per month on the unpaid amount.

## 5. Exemptions

5.1. Rental income, management or professional fees, and the first KES 500,000 of annual turnover are not subject to TOT.

5.2. A business that registers for VAT voluntarily is no longer eligible for TOT and must switch tax regimes.

*This is a general guide for small retail businesses and does not replace direct KRA guidance or a licensed tax advisor for cases with unusual circumstances.*
