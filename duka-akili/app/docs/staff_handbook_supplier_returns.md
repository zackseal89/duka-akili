# Staff Handbook, Section 4: Handling Supplier Deliveries and Returns

**Last updated:** 2025-08-10
**Applies to:** all staff receiving deliveries from any supplier

## 4.1 Receiving a delivery

- Check the delivery note against what was actually ordered before signing anything.
- Count bags and inspect for torn or wet packaging while the driver is still present.

## 4.2 Damaged or torn stock

If any bags arrive damaged or torn, report it to the shop manager within **7 days** of delivery so it can be raised with the supplier for credit or replacement. Take a photo if possible, but a written note in the delivery log is acceptable if a phone is not available.

## 4.3 Paying delivery drivers

Some suppliers allow cash payment directly to the delivery driver against the invoice, especially for small or urgent top-up orders. Always ask for a signed receipt from the driver if paying cash on delivery.

## 4.4 Expired stock

Never accept stock that is already past its printed expiry date. Refuse the delivery at the door and note it in the delivery log.

## 4.5 Who to contact

For any delivery dispute, first contact the shop manager. If unresolved within 3 days, escalate to the owner directly.

---
*This handbook is reviewed annually. Staff should also check the specific supplier contract on file, as individual supplier terms may differ from the general guidance above.*
