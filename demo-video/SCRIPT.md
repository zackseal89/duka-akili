## Why this structure (revised from the original plan)

The original script assumed a discount-calculation and Kiswahili-query demo. Neither
was captured on camera, so this cut is built entirely from what's real: the empty-state
tour, the conflict-detection centerpiece (recorded separately, the strongest material in
the whole set), the honest partial-match answer on an off-script query, and the live
document-upload + indexing pipeline. Nothing here is reenacted or implied that didn't
actually happen on screen.
